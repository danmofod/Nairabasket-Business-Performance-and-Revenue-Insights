# Nairabasket-Business-Performance-and-Revenue-Insights

## Project Background
NairaBasket is a fictional e-commerce business operating across multiple product categories, customer segments, geographic regions and order channels.
The business generates substantial transaction volume, but management requires greater visibility into the quality of revenue, profitability, customer purchasing behavior and operational performance.
The initial business concern was centered around revenue performance: What is driving changes in revenue, and are reported sales translating into sustainable commercial value?
To address this, I developed a Power BI business intelligence solution.

## Project Objective
The project was designed to provide the management with visibility in four key areas;
* financial performance of the business
* commercial performance
* customer behaviour
* operational performance

### Tools and skills Used

## Executive Summary
Insight 1-
NairaBasket's overall performance indicates that the business is generating significant commercial activity, but the quality and sustainability of that revenue require closer management attention.
The business generates approximately ₦1.8B in Net Revenue from ₦1.9B in Gross Revenue, indicating a measurable gap between initial order value and realized revenue. At the same time, COGS of approximately ₦2.6B results in a reported Gross Profit of -₦874.9M and Gross Margin of -49.5% in the overall view. This indicates that the primary commercial challenge is not simply generating sales, but ensuring that sales translate into economically sustainable revenue after accounting for product costs.
The dashboard further shows that NairaBasket's financial performance is influenced by several interconnected commercial and operational factors. Discounting, customer purchasing behavior, product/category mix, regional performance, returns, refunds and order outcomes all affect the amount and quality of revenue ultimately realized. For example, the dashboard tracks approximately 45K active customers, ₦7.07K AOV, ₦35.61K revenue per customer and a return rate of approximately 10.2%.
Consequently, management should shift from monitoring revenue as a standalone KPI toward a broader commercial performance framework that evaluates revenue alongside margin, customer economics, discount effectiveness and revenue leakage. The dashboard provides the analytical foundation for identifying where commercial value is being created, where it is being lost, and which areas warrant deeper investigation.
### Business implication
Management should prioritize a product level profitability review, examining:
- Selling price versus cost price
- Category contribution
- Discount intensity
- Sales volume
- Product mix
This would help distinguish between high revenue products and high value products and determine whether pricing, sourcing or assortment decisions are contributing to the profitability pressure.

Insight 2 -
The dashboard records approximately 45K active customers, ₦35.61K revenue per customer and ₦7.07K AOV, providing a customer-economics perspective on revenue generation. Differences in customer-segment performance and repeat purchasing behavior indicate that revenue performance should be examined in terms of customer acquisition, purchasing frequency and basket value, rather than simply total customer count.
At the operational level, the dashboard records a return rate of approximately 10.2% in the displayed period and distinguishes between delivered, cancelled, failed and returned orders. These outcomes matter because an order entering the system does not automatically translate into successfully realized revenue. Returns and refunds can reverse sales while also creating additional fulfillment and customer-service costs.
Discounting is another part of realizing revenue. The dashboard tracks a discount rate of approximately 7.5%, allowing management to investigate whether promotional activity is generating enough incremental demand or repeat purchasing to justify the reduction in realized revenue.

## Recommendation
1. Conduct a product level margin investigation.
- This would identify products generating sales but producing weak or negative contribution margins.
2. Review pricing and discount effectiveness.
- Products with high discount rates should be assessed to determine whether the additional demand generated compensates for the revenue concession.
3. Establish a returns reduction program.
- High return SKUs should be investigated for potential issues involving product quality, product descriptions, sizing/specifications.
4. Strengthen customer rentention analytics.
- This action would allow NairaBasket to distinguish revenue growth driven by customer acquisition from growth driven by customer retention and increased purchasing frequency.

### Conclusion
The NairaBasket analysis demonstrates that revenue is only one component of e-commerce business performance. But the key analytical success for a business should be that a growing or stable revenue line does not necessarily indicate a healthy commercial model. Revenue must be evaluated alongside COGS, gross margin, customer economics, discounting, returns and operational performance to determine whether growth is sustainable and profitable.
